About

About Me

Bridging quantitative rigor and business strategy

I connect business and data to turn uncertainty into concrete decisions. I'm in the final year of a BA in Business Administration & Management with a minor in Data Science at ESIC University (9.63/10 GPA, Excellence Award, Honour Roll), now focused on Machine Learning and applied AI.

As a Strategic Data Analyst Intern at Management Solutions, I worked in the Advanced Analytics team of a Tier-1 Spanish bank, analysing the SME segment across all of the group's markets. My main project was measuring the impact of a predictive credit model through an A/B test, processing millions of records on AWS. The results were inconclusive, and instead of assuming our analysis was wrong, I organised and led a meeting with the Risk team to trace where the data came from. We found an error in one of the source tables that explained the results. I then took sole ownership of the follow-up: I analysed the impact of that error, found further errors in other tables and presented the final report to the client. It taught me to always ask what a decision is really resting on.

Management Solutions offered me a full-time position conditional on completing my degree — the clearest sign that the trust they placed in me paid off.

I enjoy problems where strategy, finance and data meet, and I'm looking to bring that to strategy and data consulting in London or Spain.
